# CH-058: Promotion archetype is artifact-only with no card support

- **Category:** Game Design — Content
- **Severity:** low
- **Status:** Open

## Locations

## Problem

The promotion archetype is artifact-only with no card support, and is hard-countered by Boss C's no-promotion rule.

## Recommendation

Add card-based promotion support.

## Proposed Content

Proposed card — Sprint (Cost 2 Common: empower a pawn with a one-time 2-square forward step from any rank — card-based promotion acceleration).
