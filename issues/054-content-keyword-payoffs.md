# CH-054: Most keywords have one enabler and no payoff card

- **Category:** Game Design — Content
- **Severity:** medium
- **Status:** Open

## Locations

- `docs/cards/keywords.md`
- `src/game/cards/data/`

## Problem

Most keywords have one enabler and no payoff card — synergy lines are stubs (Freeze/Root/Disarm/Compel/Downgrade/Haste each = 1 card).

## Recommendation

Add payoff cards that reward inflicting each status, turning stubs into real archetypes.

## Proposed Content

Proposed cards — Quarantine (Cost 5 Uncommon: destroy an enemy non-king that is Frozen/Rooted/Disarmed — a 2-card removal combo, also fills the empty cost-5 slot); Stack Smash (Cost 4 Uncommon: Compel an enemy; if it has no legal capture, also Disarm it next turn).
