# CH-035: Overclock+ is undercosted

- **Category:** Game Design — Balance
- **Severity:** medium
- **Status:** Open

## Locations

- `src/game/cards/data/overclock.json`

## Problem

Overclock+ becomes `lightning-piece, actions:2`, dropping the Disarm: two unconditional capture-enabling extra moves for 4 energy at common, chainable with capture-energy refunds.

## Recommendation

Keep the Disarm on the upgrade, raise cost to 5-6, or cap to 1 capture action.
