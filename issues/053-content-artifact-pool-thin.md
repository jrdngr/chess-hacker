- **Severity:** high
- **Status:** Open

## Problem

Artifact pool is 14 vs a 26-40 target; only 2 rares (target 6-10); whole domains (sacrifice/summon/spatial/status triggers) are absent.

## Recommendation

Author ~12-26 more artifacts, weighted toward rares and the missing trigger domains.

## Proposed Content

Proposed artifacts — Antivirus/Rate Limiter (Uncommon: 3rd+ card each turn costs +2); Cold Storage (Uncommon: energy cap 30, carry ½ unspent up to 5); Loadbalancer (Rare: center-majority draw/discount); Scavenger (Uncommon: on sacrifice gain 2 energy, new on-self-sacrifice hook); Hive Mind (Rare: summon cards −2, first summoned pawn gets Shield); Dead Drop (Uncommon: first Trap/Wall draws, trap-freeze +1 turn).
