# Revival

Cards that return one of your **discarded** cards (already played or force-discarded, sitting in the discard pile) back to your hand. Cards in the **consumed pile** (those that had the Consume keyword when played) are not eligible — once consumed, they are gone for the match.

## Restore from Backup

- **Cost:** 2 energy
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Type:** Resource
- **Keywords:** **Consume**
- **Target:** None (UI prompt for choice)
- **Effect:** A modal opens showing every card in your discard pile; pick one to return to your hand. Restore from Backup itself is consumed for the match.

Targeted revival. The cost 2 reflects that the card is one-shot (Consume) and gone for the match after one use; cheap enough to splash into combo lines that need a specific tool back. Restore from Backup has Consume, so you can't loop it indefinitely — at most one targeted rescue per match per copy in the deck.

## Why it exists

Revival lets the player retrieve a card that was lost to forced discard or played earlier. With persistent hands and forced end-of-turn discards, you may sometimes have to discard a valuable card you couldn't afford to play yet. Restore from Backup is the "I want that card back *now*" tool — chosen, one-shot, cheap.

Cards with Consume (Format C:, Remote Access, Restore from Backup itself) are *not* in the discard pile after they've been played; they're in the consumed pile and unreachable by revival. Consume is the contract for "this card is finite per match."
