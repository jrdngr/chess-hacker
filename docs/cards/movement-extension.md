# Movement-extension cards

Movement-extension cards give one of your pieces an additional movement pattern. They keep the piece's normal moveset and add a new option on top.

See [`keywords.md`](keywords.md) for keyword definitions, especially **Empower**.

## Crooked Path

- **Cost:** 1
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Target:** own piece
- **Effect:** Target own piece: **Empower** with knight L-shape as an additional movement pattern this turn.
- **Design note:** the introductory Empower card — turns any piece into a temporary knight. Most interesting on rooks and bishops (suddenly threatens forks) and on pawns (a one-turn jump break). The empowered move is a knight L, so it ignores blockers like a real knight.

## Pawn Soul

- **Cost:** 1
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Target:** own piece
- **Effect:** Target own piece: **Empower** with pawn movement (one square forward in your direction, plus diagonal-forward captures) **this turn**. Lost if you move a different piece first. **Consume.**
- **Design note:** the cheap Soul. Most niche on pieces that already cover those squares (kings, queens) but useful on a knight or rook that needs a quiet step or a melee capture without giving up its line. No double-push, no en passant, no promotion — those stay pawn-only.

## Knight Soul

- **Cost:** 3
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Target:** own piece
- **Effect:** Target own piece: **Empower** with the knight L-shape as an additional movement pattern **this turn**. Lost if you move a different piece first. **Consume.**
- **Design note:** the burst counterpart to Crooked Path — costs more, consumes, and is single-use. The "use it or lose it" clause means you can't pre-buff a piece on turn N to hold for turn N+1; commit and swing.

## Bishop Soul

- **Cost:** 3
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Target:** own piece
- **Effect:** Target own piece: **Empower** with diagonal sliding as an additional movement pattern **this turn**. Lost if you move a different piece first. **Consume.**
- **Design note:** turns a rook into a queen for a turn, gives a knight the long diagonals, lets a pawn cut across the board. No-op on bishops and queens (they already have it).

## Rook Soul

- **Cost:** 4
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Target:** own piece
- **Effect:** Target own piece: **Empower** with straight sliding as an additional movement pattern **this turn**. Lost if you move a different piece first. **Consume.**
- **Design note:** the heaviest single-pattern Soul. Turns a bishop into a queen, gives a knight long files and ranks. The 4-cost matches the previous summon-rook tuning so it remains a once-per-match swing card. No-op on rooks and queens.

## Queen Soul

- **Cost:** 4
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Target:** own piece
- **Effect:** Target own piece: **Empower** with **both** straight and diagonal sliding (i.e. queen movement) as additional movement patterns **this turn**. Lost if you move a different piece first. **Consume.**
- **Design note:** the apex Soul. A pawn or knight briefly threatens the entire board. Same cost as Rook Soul because the single-use clause keeps it from running away — you commit one turn and you're done.
