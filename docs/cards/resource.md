# Resource cards

Resource cards generate energy, draw cards, or manipulate the deck. They don't act on the board directly — they fuel everything else.

See [`keywords.md`](keywords.md) for keyword definitions.

## Reconnaissance

- **Cost:** 1
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Target:** none
- **Effect:** Draw 2 cards.
- **Design note:** the workhorse draw card. Net +1 card in hand for 1 energy — pays for itself if either drawn card is worth playing. Two copies in the starter so the player almost always has draw available.

## Foresight

- **Cost:** 0
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Target:** none
- **Effect:** Look at the top 3 cards of your deck; put them back in any order.
- **Design note:** the only 0-cost card in the new pool. Doesn't draw — *fixes* what you'll draw. Not in the starter (held for drafts/shop) because its value depends on the player understanding what's in their deck. Strong with Reconnaissance: scry then draw.
