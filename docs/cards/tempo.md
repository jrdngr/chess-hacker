# Tempo cards

Tempo cards give pieces extra moves, chained moves, or free moves. They generate raw board-action without changing rules or geography.

See [`keywords.md`](keywords.md) for keyword definitions.

## Quickstep

- **Cost:** 2
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Target:** own piece
- **Effect:** Target own piece gains **Haste** this turn.
- **Design note:** the no-capture rider lives on Haste itself, so Quickstep reads as a clean positional tempo card — useful for re-positioning a slider into a fork or escaping a pin without trading.
