# Burst cards

Burst cards radically change the board: extra actions, board-wide effects, or game-ending swings. They are high-cost and typically **Consume** — one-shot moments rather than reusable tools.

See [`keywords.md`](keywords.md) for keyword definitions.

## Lightning Strike

- **Cost:** 4
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Target:** own piece
- **Effect:** Target own piece may take **2 actions** this turn. Each action may be a move or a capture. **Consume.**
- **Design note:** the win-condition card. Unlike Haste (which forbids captures), Lightning Strike lets the targeted piece move and then capture, or capture and then move, or capture twice. Designed for moments like "queen takes pawn, queen takes rook" or "rook seizes the open file, then takes the bishop." Costs the full turn budget so it dominates the turn it's played; consume keeps it from looping.
